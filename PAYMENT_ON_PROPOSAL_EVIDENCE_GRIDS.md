# Payment on proposals: evidence grids

## Grid 1. Proposal approval as the payment trigger, 2026 (EnjoyHQ)

| Source | Date | Segment / role | What they said |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zane Bennett | 2026-07-10 | Established GC, $2 to 4.99M | "The start of a project for us is when they sign the builder contract as then we can request a deposit (another thing not handled well by BT) and start a schedule of the job." Tagged to Lead Proposals. |
| Kayla Rodriguez | 2026-06-30 | Builder, does the invoicing | Creates all 10 draws by copy-paste from the signed proposal PDF, saves them as drafts, releases only the deposit at lead-to-job conversion. |
| Chuck Regan | 2026-06-17 | Cabinet / semi-custom | "We take 50% down as a deposit and then 50% upon delivery... you would select Schedule A... It would load onto the contract. And then once that contract is signed and enters our production pipeline..." |
| David Lipsky | 2026-06-25 | Design-build | Design retainer invoiced at 100% first, then "when they approve the big project, I invoice 25% of the labor as a deposit." |
| Alex Dorfman | 2026-08-12 | Metal fabricator | 50/50 under $100k, 10-15-15-60 over. Deposit is a percent of contract price, pulled from Buildertrend. |
| Michael Hill | 2026-06-23 | Builder | Collects a 50% deposit; currently at risk of double-billing between allowances and approved selections. |
| Vik Terala | 2026-02-12 | Builder (selections interview) | 5% deposit taken at quick-quote signoff, then "we go into our contract proposal." |
| Lincoln Alexander | 2026-05-24 | In-app feedback, estimates | "Ability to automate sending deposit link after estimate is approved." |
| Adam Peters | 2026-04-27 | Remodeler | Will not book subs until "I've got a deposit from this person." |
| Ryan Schwarz | 2026-07-06 | Custom builder | Takes a design deposit to get drafting started. |
| Lauren Farris, Renew Design and Build | 2026-07-07 | Org owner, in-app feedback | Deposit to start design work, balance when design completes, which can run up to a year. Wants a no-terms / no-due-date option so it does not read as overdue. |
| Claire Wigler | 2026-04-11 | Salesforce feedback | Wants multiple proposals per lead, accepted "with deposits." Currently creates three separate leads to do it. |
| Amy Ross (write-up of the above) | 2026-04-14 | Note, High Priority | "Deposits are collected per scope." One accepted proposal per lead forces Smith Kitchen / Smith Bathroom / Smith Addition, which corrupts job costing and time-clock allocation. |
| Kimberly Handler | 2026-04-01 | Product idea | "We request a deposit from the client that is applied pro-rata to the ORIGINAL job only. It is NOT applied to change orders." |

## Grid 4. Out of scope: lead-stage money is a different object

| Participant | Study | The money | Why it is not draw 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chad White | Link draw to schedule, 2025 | Pre-construction cost charged when the pre-construction agreement is signed, $1,500 on a job where $10,000 was already collected | Charged during the pre-sale job, before a build contract exists |
| Jasmine Robinson | Fixed price, 2025 | "Payment 1: Design agreement requested at the beginning of job. Fee. Non-refundable." | Separate from her material deposit, which is the contract-price one |
| Ryan Knibbe | Fixed price, 2025 | Separate contract for the design fee | "If customer moves forward, its applied to job. If they don't move forward with job its a fee taken by builder." |
| Andrea Weig | Fixed price and link draw, 2025 | Non-refundable deposit to create a plan or quote | "Doesn't require payment at proposal signing." On the pre-sale job, "won't have contract price yet... Only have TBD price," so a percent is not computable |

## Grid 5. Disconfirming cases

| Participant | Date | Why it cuts against the design |
|---|---|---|
| Andrea Weig | 2025 | Takes no money at proposal signing at all. Her $10,000 lands at the pre-con meeting. The control must stay optional. |
| Hilda Garcia | 2025 | Her company ignores the proposal's payment terms and invoices at owner discretion based on payroll needs. Structure set at proposal time gets overridden. |
| Jared Glick | 2025 | Does not want invoices created before he is ready to send them, for clutter and staleness. Hand-adds sales tax and custom description, so he asked for a notification to review. Argues for stage-and-notify over auto-send. |
| David Huck | 2025 | "Cali law doesn't allow deposits." Percent-of-contract deposits face statutory caps in some states, so the flat option is not just a convenience. |
